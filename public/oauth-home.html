Tilia is a web application for mental health professionals to manage patients, schedule sessions, write clinical notes, track payments, and send reminders.

  • Patient and clinical record management
  • Session scheduling and reminders
  • Payment tracking and collection follow-up
  • Optional Google Calendar integration
